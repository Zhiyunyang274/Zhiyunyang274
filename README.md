## Data & decision systems · 数据与决策系统

| Project | Problem → approach | Access |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[QianPulse · 黔脉](https://github.com/Zhiyunyang274/qianpulse)** | Bridge screening from vehicle IMU signals: aggregate noisy crossings into frequency fingerprints and track baseline shifts. | [Code](https://github.com/Zhiyunyang274/qianpulse) · [Demo](https://qianpulse.caixuntong.cn) |
| **TIDE** | Logistics capacity forecasting and rebalancing: predict supply–demand imbalances 1–4 hours ahead, then plan repositioning with constrained optimization. | Private project |

## About & approach

Computer Science and Technology at **South China University of Technology**, with a minor in Finance. Independent Apple developer, hackathon builder, and **WorldQuant Global Challenge Top 100**.

I focus on tool-using agents, local software, and noisy real-world data. Across these projects, I aim to make actions visible, give consequential operations clear boundaries, keep failures recoverable, and distinguish measured evidence from simulation.

### 你好，我是云扬

华南理工大学计算机科学与技术，辅修金融；独立 Apple 开发者，已有多款 App 上架 App Store；黑客松选手，WorldQuant Global Challenge Top 100。

### 项目方向

- **数据与决策系统**：黔脉融合过桥车辆 IMU 信号，为桥梁检查优先级提供参考；TIDE 预测公路港供需失衡并优化跨港调车；FlowCT 从车辆事件流反演节点服务能力，串联异常检测、根因定位、传播预报与反事实干预；PolySignal Pro 面向市场情报与模拟交易。
- **AI 智能体**：Linux-Agent 连接自然语言、运维执行与风险检查；飞书聊天机器人探索长期记忆、多用户隔离与个性化对话。
- **应用与实用工具**：MacKit 原生菜单栏工具箱、财讯通 2.0、浏览器本地 Word 转 Excel，以及回线、静默点名、QDeciso 的产品支持与隐私入口。

TIDE、FlowCT 目前为私有项目；验证基于合成仿真数据，不代表真实业务收益。

### 做事方式

让动作可见，让风险有边界，让失败可恢复，让数据和结论可追溯。
